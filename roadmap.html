Roadmap · Supply chain AI

From demand to delivery: what we're designing next.

We're reviewing requirements for two supply chain systems: OTIF Predictability and Demand Forecasting. Neither is built or available yet. This page sets out the problems we're scoping and the capabilities we expect to design for, so supply chain teams can help shape them.

Status: requirements review

In requirements review

OTIF Predictability

Predict late or incomplete orders before they happen, so teams can step in early instead of reacting after a missed delivery.

Capabilities in scope

  • OTIF risk prediction for open orders
  • Order prioritization by strategic importance, commitments, revenue and service levels
  • Delivery risk alerts for inventory, production, transport and supplier issues
  • Root cause and exception analytics for recurring OTIF failures
  • Next-best-action recommendations to recover at-risk orders
  • A real-time control tower for orders, fulfillment and exceptions

In requirements review

Demand Forecasting

Forecast demand with AI and machine learning, and turn those forecasts into better inventory, replenishment and allocation decisions.

Capabilities in scope

  • Demand forecasts linked to inventory, production and distribution data
  • Inventory availability intelligence
  • Replenishment recommendations from demand patterns, lead times and supply constraints
  • Allocation recommendations across warehouses, distribution centers and customer locations
  • Supplier and logistics performance insight

The outcomes we're designing for

These are design goals, not measured results.

Higher OTIF performance

More reliable, more complete deliveries.

Customer satisfaction

Better service levels and customer experience.

Lower supply chain costs

Less expediting, rework and avoidable logistics cost.

Operational resilience

Fulfillment risks found and resolved earlier.

How we're getting there

  1. Now

    Requirements review

    Working through use cases, data sources and success measures.

  2. Next

    Data & integration

    Assessing order, inventory, production and logistics data.

  3. Then

  4. Later

    Governed build

    Production build on AEGIS, with safety, governance and ethics controls.
