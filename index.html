The foundation · AEGIS

Safety, governance and ethics are built in, not added later.

AEGIS is cloud-agnostic governance middleware. It sits between your models and your users and decides, for every output, whether to pass, modify, escalate or block it, and it records why.

How AEGIS works

AI Safety

Guardrails, human-approval gates and escalation paths for high-risk outputs.

AI Governance

Policy-as-code, append-only audit logs and per-run cost attribution.

AI Ethics

SHAP/LIME explainability, conformal prediction and PII redaction, so decisions can be explained and defended.
