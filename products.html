01 · Healthcare AI assistant

OpenHealth Guide

Trusted health guidance in the patient's own language. OpenHealth Guide handles emergency triage, medication questions and evidence-based guidance, each through a specialized agent, with automated safety disclaimers on every response.

Built on AWS Bedrock AgentCore with a RAG knowledge base of clinical guidelines, drug databases and treatment protocols. Real-time translation uses Meta NLLB-200 with medical terminology validation. HIPAA-ready by design: zero PHI storage, TLS 1.3 and immutable audit logging.

03 · Multimodal emotion AI

Psychodynamic AI

Emotion-aware conversation, in real time. Psychodynamic AI reads facial action units from live video and fuses them with voice prosody and text sentiment to support empathetic, CBT/DBT-grounded conversations.

A confidence-weighted fusion layer runs the full inference path in-process, the same constraint profile as edge deployment. Crisis detection, longitudinal session memory and HIPAA-compliant end-to-end encryption are built in.
