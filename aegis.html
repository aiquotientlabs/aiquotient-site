AEGIS · The foundation

AI safety, governance and ethics for every model output.

AEGIS is cloud-agnostic governance middleware. It reviews each output before it reaches a user, explains the decision, and keeps a record that auditors and regulators can follow.

Request an AEGIS briefing

Four verdicts. Every output.

AEGIS scores each response against your policies and returns one clear decision.

PASS

The output meets policy and is released as written.

MODIFY

The output is corrected first, for example by redacting PII or adding a required disclaimer.

ESCALATE

A person reviews it through a human-approval gate before anything is released.

BLOCK

The output breaks a rule and never reaches the user. The reason is logged.
